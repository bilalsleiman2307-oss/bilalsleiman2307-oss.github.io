Fast door opening when you are locked out in Berlin

A lockout usually starts with one detail: did the door simply close behind you, or was it locked with a key? That distinction affects the opening method and the price. When you call, give us the Berlin address, the type of door and anything unusual about the lock. We can then prepare for the actual situation instead of making assumptions at the door.

For a shut apartment door that has not been locked, the daytime price starts at €59 including VAT. This type of door is opened without damage. A locked, damaged or defective door may require a different method, so we do not promise damage-free opening for every possible condition. The fixed price for the described job and the travel charge are agreed by phone before the technician sets off.

Trust is available around the clock in Berlin. Depending on the address and traffic, arrival usually takes around 10–30 minutes. The estimate is given for your location when you contact us; congestion, roadworks and current call-outs can affect the journey.

Frequently asked questions about locksmith service in Berlin

How much does a door opening cost in Berlin?

A shut, unlocked door starts at €59 from 7 am to 8 pm, €79 from 8 pm to midnight and €99 from midnight to 7 am. All displayed prices include VAT.

Can you open a shut door without damage?

Yes. We open a door without damage when it has only shut and has not been locked with a key. Locked or damaged doors require an individual assessment.

How quickly can you arrive?

Depending on the Berlin address and traffic, arrival usually takes around 10–30 minutes. We give you an estimate for your location when you call.

Is Trust available 24/7?

Yes. The emergency locksmith service is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays.

Does a key left inside cost more?

No. A key left or inserted on the inside does not increase our door-opening price. Tell us where the key is when you call.

What if my key is lost or broken?

We can open the door and inspect the lock. A cylinder or lock is changed only when necessary and after you approve the work and price.

Is a new cylinder included in the opening price?

No. Profile cylinders and other materials are separate from the door-opening price and are installed only after explicit agreement.

What proof do I need for the door opening?

Access authorization is checked before or immediately after opening. An ID, tenancy evidence or another suitable confirmation may be used.

Need a locksmith in Berlin now?

Call or use WhatsApp and provide your Berlin address, door condition and key problem. We clarify the fixed price and travel charge before setting off.
